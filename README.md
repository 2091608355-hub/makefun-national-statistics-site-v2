China by Numbers puts China's 2024 official statistics in one small offline site: 1,408.3 million people, GDP of ¥134.9 trillion growing 5.0%, GDP per person of ¥95,749, life expectancy of 79.0 years and 67.0% of people living in cities. Five pages — Overview, Economy, People, Regions and Living — cover the headline numbers, a GDP chart you can redraw from any starting year, age and industry breakdowns, and a searchable, sortable table of the ten largest provinces. It is plain static files, runs on desktop and phone, and fetches nothing from the network.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. The hand-in program is `node main.ts` (see `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`).
